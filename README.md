### Hey, I'm Chimezie, but most people call me ChimeXD.

I'm a **design engineer**: I work between design and code, building AI products that feel human.

I spent six years as a product designer with teams at **Deel**, **Foundation**, **Chedda Finance**, **Bulan** and
**Appmakers**, where I'm a Senior Product Designer today. Over time my work moved closer to code. I don't stop at the
handoff: I design the interface, set up the type, tokens and components that hold it together, and build it.

AI is part of how I work too. I use Claude to stress-test UI logic, build state machines and get from idea to
deployment faster.

#### What I've been building

- **[Meridian](https://github.com/ChimeXXD/meridian)**: a free iPhone app that tells you exactly when your next
  episode comes out, in your own time zone, with widgets and a release-night countdown. SwiftUI, open source.
- **[Clepso](https://clepso-app.vercel.app)**: a local-first macOS menu bar app that shows how much of your Claude Code
  and Codex usage limits you've used, when they reset and how fast you're going.
